## 1. Introduction

StudyHub is a modern web-based Learning Management System (LMS) designed to provide a centralized platform for managing educational activities such as courses, learning materials, assignments, quizzes, announcements, and student progress.

This document describes the long-term vision, mission, purpose, target users, and expected impact of the StudyHub platform.

---

## 2. Why StudyHub Exists

In many educational institutions, learning resources are spread across multiple platforms such as:

* WhatsApp
* Email
* Google Drive
* Excel Sheets
* Printed Notes

Because these tools are disconnected, students often miss important information, teachers repeatedly share the same files, and administrators struggle to track academic activities effectively.

StudyHub exists to solve this problem by providing **one secure, organized, and scalable platform** where all learning activities can be managed from a single location.

---

## 3. Problem We Want to Solve

### Current Situation

Students:

* Cannot easily find notes and assignments.
* Miss deadlines due to scattered communication.
* Have no centralized progress tracking.

Teachers:

* Spend time repeatedly sharing files.
* Manage assignments manually.
* Lack a single platform for course management.

Administrators:

* Have limited visibility into student engagement.
* Find it difficult to generate reports.
* Use multiple tools to manage academic operations.

### Our Solution

StudyHub will provide:

* Centralized course management
* Online assignment submission
* Quiz and assessment support
* Progress tracking
* Role-based access control
* Secure cloud-based storage
